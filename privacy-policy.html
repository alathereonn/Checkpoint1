Privacy Policy — Checkpoint

Effective date: November 24, 2026
Android application ID: id.ac.itk.checkpoint
Developer: Checkpoint developer (alathereonn)

Checkpoint is a game backlog tracker. This policy explains how Checkpoint handles information when you use the Android app.

1. Information stored on your device

Checkpoint saves your game library and backlog status, game metadata, daily quest titles and descriptions, quest status, and play-session dates, times and durations in a local SQLite database. Game images may also be cached on your device. Do not include sensitive personal information in quest text.

The app does not require an account or login. Its current implementation does not send your saved backlog, quests or session history to a Checkpoint-operated server. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android or your backup provider may back up app data separately.

2. Internet access and third-party services

Checkpoint uses the RAWG Video Games Database API to load game lists, search results, details and available age-rating information. Your game search terms and the game identifiers you request are sent to RAWG as part of those requests. RAWG and its network providers may receive your IP address, request time and other technical request information needed to deliver and protect their service.

Game images are loaded from URLs supplied by RAWG. These image requests may go to RAWG or other image hosts, which may receive your IP address and request information. Their data practices are governed by their own policies. Read the RAWG Privacy Policy. Opening a RAWG link in your browser is subject to the browser's and RAWG's policies.

Checkpoint uses HTTPS for RAWG API requests. The image URLs are supplied by third parties, and the security and availability of those hosts are outside the developer's control.

3. Permissions and notifications

  • Internet: to retrieve game information and images and open external links.
  • Notifications: on supported Android versions, the app may request permission to display a user-started play-session timer notification. You can manage notification permissions in Android settings.
  • Foreground service: to keep a user-started play-session timer running and display its notification while you use other apps.

4. Retention and deletion

Locally stored library, quest and session data remains on your device until you remove it or clear the app's storage. You can delete quests in the app. To remove all local Checkpoint data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. This also removes your locally saved backlog and history.

Device or cloud backups, if enabled, may retain copies independently; manage or delete those backups through your backup provider. RAWG and image hosts may retain their own request logs according to their policies; Checkpoint does not control those records.

5. Security

Checkpoint stores its database in the app's local storage, protected by Android's app sandbox under normal device conditions. Network requests to the RAWG API use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security; protect your device and avoid entering sensitive information in quest text.

6. Children's privacy and game content

Checkpoint displays information and images from an external game catalogue, which may include mature content. The app may show an ESRB game rating when RAWG supplies one; missing ratings do not mean a game is suitable for all ages. Checkpoint does not currently filter games or images by age rating. A game's rating is different from the app's own content rating.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
